Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Noises mobile application ("Noises", the "App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information. Your privacy matters to us, and Noises is designed to work entirely on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Noises does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. We do not require an account, and the App does not include analytics or advertising trackers. The audio you hear is generated on your device and is never recorded or uploaded.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

To remember where you left off, the App saves your preferences locally on your device, including:

  • Your last selected noise color
  • Your last timer duration
  • Your per-color sound tuning settings

This data stays on your device, is not shared with us or any third party, and is removed if you delete the App.

3. Permissions

The App requests only the permissions needed to play audio (for example, audio playback and, on some platforms, a foreground service so playback continues in the background). On iOS, AirPlay routing uses the system audio route picker to send audio to devices you choose. These permissions are used solely to provide playback and are not used to collect data.

4. Third-Party Services

The App itself does not integrate third-party data-collection services. If you download the App from an app store (such as the Apple App Store or Google Play), that store's own privacy practices apply to your download and payment, and are governed by their respective policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Noises does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the App collects no personal data, it does not create profiles of users of any age.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes become effective when we post the updated policy, indicated by the "Last updated" date above.

7. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at noisesapp@gmail.com.
